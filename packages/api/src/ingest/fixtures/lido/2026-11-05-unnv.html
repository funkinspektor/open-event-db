UNNV

Six years of silence — and then “ABIBOK” drops. An idler who refuses to be ruled by time. Dark beats, viscous rhymes and that unmistakable chemistry between the members — this is UNNV. There are no random lines here — every one cuts straight through. Three voices. One standard. A language that grates on the ear with its brutal honesty. If you haven’t heard them yet — press play. If you have — give it another listen.


All the best tracks are coming this autumn.
