TWILIGHT - Party

Twilight Party! 🖤

An entire night of the best of the Twilight soundtrack and other songs with the same vibe: Dark Pop, Emo, Indie & 2000s Bangers!


+ video highlights projected onto the walls
+ plenty of fog
+ Berlin’s biggest disco ball
+ 2 bars, chill area, smoking area


Let yourself be enchanted by the dark atmosphere and unique sound of the Twilight universe. Get ready for a night of melancholic indie anthems, iconic 2000s classics, and the mysterious aesthetic of Forks.


Whether you’re Team Edward, Team Jacob, or simply here for great music and a magical night – this is where nostalgia, darkness, and the dance floor come together.


Vampire, werewolf, or simply dark fantasy: Costumes are highly encouraged.
Doors open and the party starts at the witching hour (11:59 PM) – make sure you arrive on time!
