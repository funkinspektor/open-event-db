In November 2024, Norwegian band Mayflower Madame launched their highly anticipated third LP'Insight', which saw the band refining their signature blend of pulsating post-punk and shimmering psych-gaze into a sharper and more expansive sonic landscape than ever before.
The album has garnered much acclaim and extensive radio support, including MOJO, Rolling Stone Germany, Eclipsed Magazine, Sonic Seducer, Rumore Magazine, BBC 6 Music and many more. Known for their intense and atmospheric live performances, Mayflower Madame has headlined shows in fourteen different countries since the release, and more tours and festivals await this autumn in support of a new "Deluxe Version" of 'Insight' (launched July 19th and featuring previously unreleased bonus tracks).

BLKE
BLKE is a Berlin-based psych-noise rock band operating at the intersection of krautrock, noise rock,psychedelia and drone. Led by German multidisciplinary artist Jakob Buraczewski and featuring members of Camera, the band combines hypnotic repetition, motorik propulsion and abrasive sonictextures into a sound that is both immersive and emotionally charged.
In May 2026, BLKE opened Desertfest Berlin and released their debut album via Tonzonen Records. The album was followed by the band’s first European tour, taking their music across Germany, the Czech Republic, Austria and Poland.
