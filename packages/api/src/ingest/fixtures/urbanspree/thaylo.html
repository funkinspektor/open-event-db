About the Concert

After Dublin, Brussels and Paris, the journey continues in Berlin. A melodic house night built around a carefully curated lineup, where every set is a chapter of the same story. For his first appearance in Berlin, Thaylo brings his immersive sound to Urban Spree alongside Darin Epsilon and Gareden. Deep, emotional melodies, slow-building tension and an elegant energy that holds the room until the very last track.
