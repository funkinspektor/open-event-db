About the Concert

Pure Obsessions & Red Nights
Support: Nico Amara
Doors: 20H
Urban Spree, Revaler Strasse 99, 10245 Berlin
Tickets 21€/ Early birds 17€
 
Pure Obsessions & Red Nights return to the European road in support of their new album, Refined Obsessions.
At the crossroads of darkwave, nocturnal 80s new wave and industrial rock, the band brings together the dark elegance of Depeche Mode, the rock drive of Billy Idol and the emotional intensity of 90s alternative rock.
The Refined Obsessions Tour 2027 will celebrate both the future and the roots of the band’s universe, featuring new material from Refined Obsessions and The Night Trilogy, alongside 80s classics from the 2026 EP Red Nights Covers — a tribute to the artists and era that shaped their sound.
Nico Amara will open the night with a dark, intense and emotionally charged set. Blending atmospheric textures with raw energy, her sound creates the perfect entrance into the world of Pure Obsessions & Red Nights.
A powerful opening act to set the tone before the Red Nights take over.
